How to Play

You win by holding 20k or more in your hand.

Any pile can be doubled, halved, moved, or swapped with another.

Split divides all money on the table evenly between the two hands.

Shoot kills your opponent and wins the game, IF they are richer after all other moves have resolved. Each player only has one bullet. Aim wisely.

This is a simultaneous action game. While you are making your move, your opponent is making their move.

Moves play out at the same time, but there is a resolution order for different actions.

Double then Halve then Split then Move then Swap then Shoot

If Double is played on an empty pile, it adds money.

About This Game

Stash is a fun project I started to try to learn more about two things: game theory & AI.

My goal is to design a simultaneous dilemma game which is also balanced & fun to play. And then to train the strongest possible AI player for it.

Unlike turn-based games like chess, play in Stash is simultaneous. You and your opponent make your moves and play them together at the same time. Training an AI for this type of game is harder.

The current AI player is pretty strong but not world-class. If anyone out there wants to collaborate on a stronger player, contact me.

Thanks for visiting, and I hope you enjoy playing Stash.
