How to Play

First hand to reach $20,000 wins.

Each round, you and your opponent secretly pick one action. Both happen at the same time.

You can double or halve any pile, move money between any piles, swap any piles, or guard your own for a round.

Double adds to an empty pile.

Stash does not protect from Halve or Double.
